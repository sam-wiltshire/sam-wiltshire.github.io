Terrain rules and mission structure adapted from Atomic Mass Games' Star Wars: Legion Core Rulebook (eff. 4.21.2026), Recon Rulebook (eff. 4.30.2025) and Terrain Layout Cards (DOC62, eff. 07.2026). Map layouts are a fair, symmetric approximation of the official Battle Cards — not pixel-exact reproductions. Generic terrain placement (Large/Medium/Small/Scatter/Barricade) is left to players by the rules, which is exactly what this tool generates for you.
